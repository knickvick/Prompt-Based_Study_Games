PHYSICS FORMULA SHEET

Unit 3 - Forces

  • Newton's 2nd Law: $F_{\text{net}} = ma$
  • Force of Gravity: $F_g = mg \quad (g \approx 9.8 \text{ m/s}^2)$
  • Friction Force: $F_f \le \mu_s F_N \quad (\text{static}), \quad F_f = \mu_k F_N \quad (\text{kinetic})$
  • Spring Force (Hooke's Law): $F_s = -kx$
  • Centripetal Acceleration: $a_c = \frac{v^2}{r}$
  • Centripetal Force: $F_c = m a_c = m\frac{v^2}{r}$
  • Angled Forces on Incline: $F_{\parallel} = mg\sin\theta, \quad F_{\perp} = mg\cos\theta$

Unit 4 - Conservation

  • Kinetic Energy: $KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$
  • Gravitational Potential Energy: $GPE = mgh$
  • Elastic Potential Energy: $EPE = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$
  • Work-Energy Theorem: $W = \Delta E_k = F d \cos\theta$
  • Momentum: $p = mv$
  • Impulse-Momentum: $J = F \Delta t = \Delta p = m \Delta v$
  • Conservation of Momentum: $\sum p_i = \sum p_f$
  • Elastic Collisions: $m_1 v_{1i} + m_2 v_{2i} = m_1 v_{1f} + m_2 v_{2f}$
  • Inelastic Collisions: $m_1 v_{1i} + m_2 v_{2i} = (m_1 + m_2) v_f$
