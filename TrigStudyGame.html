Trig Ratios Cheatsheet

1. Primary Trigonometric Ratios (SOH-CAH-TOA)

Sine ($$\sin$$)

$$\sin \theta = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Hypotenuse}}$$

Cosine ($$\cos$$)

$$\cos \theta = \frac{\text{Adjacent}}{\text{Hypotenuse}}$$

Tangent ($$\tan$$)

$$\tan \theta = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Adjacent}}$$

2. Reciprocal Trigonometric Ratios

Cosecant ($$\csc$$)

$$\csc \theta = \frac{\text{Hyp}}{\text{Opp}} = \frac{1}{\sin\theta}$$

Secant ($$\sec$$)

$$\sec \theta = \frac{\text{Hyp}}{\text{Adj}} = \frac{1}{\cos\theta}$$

Cotangent ($$\cot$$)

$$\cot \theta = \frac{\text{Adj}}{\text{Opp}} = \frac{1}{\tan\theta}$$

3. Quadrant Signs: "All Students Take Calculus"

Quadrant I ($$0^\circ$$ to $$90^\circ$$)

All Positive

Quadrant II ($$90^\circ$$ to $$180^\circ$$)

Sine ($$\csc$$) only

Quadrant III ($$180^\circ$$ to $$270^\circ$$)

Tangent ($$\cot$$) only

Quadrant IV ($$270^\circ$$ to $$360^\circ$$)

Cosine ($$\sec$$) only
