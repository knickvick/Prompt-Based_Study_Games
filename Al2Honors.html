Trig Quick Reference

Special Right Triangles:

  • $30^\circ-60^\circ-90^\circ$: Sides ratio is $x : x\sqrt{3} : 2x$
  • $45^\circ-45^\circ-90^\circ$: Sides ratio is $x : x : x\sqrt{2}$

Unit Circle Definitions:

  • $\sin\theta = y/r$   |   $\cos\theta = x/r$
  • $\tan\theta = y/x$   |   $\csc\theta = r/y$
  • $\sec\theta = r/x$   |   $\cot\theta = x/y$

Quadrant Signs (ASTC):

Quad II (S)
Sin (+) / Cos (-)
Quad I (A)
All (+)
Quad III (T)
Tan (+) / Sin (-)
Quad IV (C)
Cos (+) / Sin (-)

Study Tip

Always find the positive acute Reference Angle ($\theta'$) to easily determine standard trig values.
